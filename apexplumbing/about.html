About us

A plumbing company in Johannesburg that starts by listening.

Apex Plumbing & Drainage Solutions is a local plumbing and drainage contractor. This project focuses on helping customers explain a problem and understand what happens next.

Our approach

Plumbing problems can interrupt a whole day. We want the first conversation to feel useful: what is happening, how urgent it may be and what information will help a plumber prepare.

We aim to explain options in everyday language, respect the home we enter and confirm the scope before work begins.

Our story

Founded in Johannesburg in 2021.

Apex Plumbing & Drainage Solutions has worked from Johannesburg, Gauteng since 2021. The business handles plumbing and drainage work for homes and small businesses, with the same plain-language approach on every job.

If you are not sure whether a job is a leak, a blockage or something else, describe what you can see and hear. That is usually enough to choose the right next step.
